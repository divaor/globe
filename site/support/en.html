Help and support

Mundora is the world on one globe: its countries and cities, its nature, its history and its sky. It is made by one person, and every question and report helps.

Ask a question or report a problem

Write to divaor@gmail.com, or on Mundora's GitHub page (it needs a free GitHub account). It helps to say which device you use, the app's language, and the version, which is at the end of Settings.

Common questions

Is Mundora free?

Yes, all of it. There are no ads, no account and nothing to buy in the app.

Does it work without an internet connection?

The apps carry all their maps, stories, games and guides, so they work offline from the first time you open them. A few things are read live and need a connection: the pictures, Wikipedia's summaries and On this day, the weather, earthquakes and the Space Station, and what lives or grows near a place. On the website, what you have opened stays available offline, and you can keep the detailed map of an area: in Settings, under Detailed terrain, Save the area on screen.

How do I take my progress to another device?

Open the round button at the top right, then Your data on another device. Save a backup file there, then open the same file on the other device. Your progress, streaks, badges, trips and settings come with it.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Each daily game has a 🔔 switch for its own reminder, and you choose the time once. Settings → Reminders shows them all and turns them all off at once.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language. Mundora speaks English, Spanish (Latin American or from Spain), Portuguese, French and German. Like my phone follows your device's language.

Why can't I hear the tours and stories read aloud?

Mundora reads with your device's own voices. If there is none for your language, add one in your device's text-to-speech settings, then choose it in Settings → Reading aloud.

Why does it ask where I am?

Only when you tap What lives here?, What grows here? or Use my location in the sky, and once in Legacies of the past, to know which country you are in. You can always say no. The privacy policy says exactly what happens to it.

How do I delete my data?

Everything is on your device and nowhere else. Uninstall the app, or clear this site's data in your browser, and it is gone.
