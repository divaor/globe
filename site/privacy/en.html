Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Mundora collects nothing about you. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device.

This policy covers the Mundora apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, and the website at divaor.github.io/globe. Mundora is made by Diego Valencia.

What stays on your device

Your settings, your progress, streaks and badges, your trips, the map areas you save, your reminders and any data of your own that you open in the app are kept on your device, in the app's own storage (or, on the website, in your browser's storage for this site). They are never sent to Mundora or to anyone else.

  • To delete them, uninstall the app, or clear this site's data in your browser.
  • A backup file (Settings → Your data on another device) is made only when you ask for one, and goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Your location

Mundora asks for your location only when you tap something that needs it, and you can say no; everything else keeps working.

  • The sky (Use my location) uses it on your device only, to show the sky above you.
  • Tonight’s sky (Use my location) uses it on your device only, to work out the Moon, the planets and the dark hours where you are. It keeps your position, rounded to about 10 km, on your device, so that it opens at your place next time.
  • Legacies of the past uses it on your device only, to know which country you are in. Only the country is kept, on your device.
  • What lives here? and What grows here? round your position to about 10 km (a tenth of a degree) and send that rounded point to iNaturalist to ask which animals and plants are seen near there.

Apart from the rounded position Tonight’s sky keeps on your device, Mundora never stores your location, and it never shares it with anyone else.

Services the app reads

Most of Mundora works with no connection. A few things are read live from public services while you use them, and only when you open the feature that shows them. As with any website, each service sees your device's internet address and what is asked for; Mundora adds no identifier, no account and no tracking code to these requests. Each service's own privacy policy applies to them.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps the internet addresses of visitors for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement. The apps do not use GitHub Pages: everything they need is inside them.

Reminders, reading aloud and sharing

  • Reminders are set on your device and shown by your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Reading aloud uses your device's own voices. Nothing is recorded or sent.
  • Sharing a result, a trip or a link uses your device's share menu and goes only where you send it.

Links to other sites

Links to Wikipedia and other sites open in your browser. What you do there is covered by those sites' own policies.

Donations

If you choose to support Mundora on the website, the payment happens on Ko-fi, under Ko-fi's privacy policy. Ko-fi tells me what it tells any creator about a donation, such as the amount and the name and message you choose to give. It is never linked to how you use the app. The apps have no donation button.

Children

Mundora is made for everyone, children included, and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever needs to work differently, this page will say so first, with a new date at the top.
